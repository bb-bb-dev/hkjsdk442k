Tubolito SYNCD on Garmin

Know your pressure.
Keep your ride.

TubeWitch for Tubolito SYNCD brings tire pressure from Tubolito SYNCD inner tubes to compatible Garmin Edge devices and watches.

See front and rear pressure together, set your own low and high pressure limits, and use popup, audible and vibration alerts on supported devices.

Get TubeWitchStart SetupExplore Settings

Your tubes. Your pressure limits.

TubeWitch reads Bluetooth LE broadcasts from your SYNCD sensors. Enter each sensor's full name in TubeWitch, choose its Front or Rear position, and set low and high pressure targets for each wheel.

  1. Wake the sensors by moving the wheels.
  2. Enter the full sensor names, such as SYNCD-C3D0, in the matching TubeWitch sensor set.
  3. Set your preferred units and pressure limits, then confirm live readings.

Follow the Tubolito setup guide

Pressure, alerts and ride data

  • Front and rear pressure together, or a single enabled sensor.
  • Separate low and high pressure targets for each wheel.
  • Popup, audible and vibration alerts on supported Garmin devices.
  • Configurable alarm delays, reset delays and additional repeats.
  • Up to five named bike or wheelset profiles with manual selection, Auto Switch and touchscreen controls.
  • Custom layouts, labels, colors and activity recording.
